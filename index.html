Public Order, Space & Storage

Maintenance Attrition Rule

Condition loss is linear with underfunding. Paying 100% of required maintenance causes 0% condition loss. Paying 75% causes 25% of the maximum loss, 50% causes 50%, 25% causes 75%, and paying 0% causes the full maximum loss. The maximum loss is configurable below.

Toll Road Rules

Seasonal Toll Factors

Base Traffic Value: the theoretical gp value of traffic using the road each turn. Increase it to represent a busier road or larger regional economy.

Toll Charge: the percentage of the traffic value collected as tolls. Increasing this raises toll revenue but, later, can be made to carry political or traffic consequences.

Seasonal Factor: multiplies road traffic for each season. 1.00 = normal traffic, 1.20 = 20% higher, 0.70 = 30% lower, 0.20 = 80% lower.

Safety Floor: the minimum fraction of normal toll revenue that can be earned from road-safety factors. A value of 0.50 means safety can never reduce the road below 50% of its potential.

Public Order / Wall Condition Weights: determine how much the keep's authority and the condition of its defensive works contribute to the road-safety score.

Formula: Base Traffic × Toll Charge × Season × Safety × Active Event Modifiers.

Village Rent Rules

Village rent income is deliberately simple for now: Base Village Income × Rent Rate. Public order does not alter the rent amount.

Keep Staff Promotion & Training Rules

Keep Staff can be hired directly at Steward, Tradesman or Artisan level to represent settlers arriving with existing skills. Promotion uses the training points and gold below. Each completed training period awards one training point.

Building Condition Buff Rules

Building effects are reduced by condition. Good and Slightly Damaged use the full effect; Damaged uses the configured percentage; Severely Damaged uses the configured percentage; Destroyed provides no effect. Staffing requirements must also be met.

Keep Staff Construction Efficiency

These are fractions of base construction time removed per worker. For example 0.15 = 15%.

Artisan Specialisations

Presets are available when hiring Artisans. Custom specialisations can add modifiers to buildings, finance and construction.
